## Sakila Practice Exercises
**Basic Retrieval**: Write a query to list the first name, last name, and email address of all customers living in the 'California' district.

**Filtering & Sorting**: Find all films that have a rating of 'PG-13', a rental_rate greater than 2.00, and a length longer than 120 minutes. Sort the results by length in descending order.

**Joining Tables:** Retrieve the titles of all films in the 'Action' category. You will need to join the film, film_category, and category tables.

**Aggregations**: Calculate the total number of films rented by each customer. Display the customer's full name and the total count, sorted by the number of rentals in descending order.

G**rouping & Having**: Identify the top 5 most popular actors by counting how many films each actor has appeared in. Display the actor's full name and the count.

**Subqueries**: Find the names of all customers who have rented a movie that stars the actor 'PENELOPE GUINESS'.

C**omplex Joins**: List the total revenue (sum of amount) generated by each store. Include the city and country where the store is located to provide context.

**Date/Time Manipulation**: Calculate the average rental_duration (the time between rental_date and return_date) for all rentals. Note: You will need to handle cases where the film has not been returned yet.

**Conditional Logic (CASE statement):** Create a report showing the title of each film and a column labeled 'Rental Status' that displays 'Cheap' if the rental_rate is less than 2.00, 'Moderate' if between 2.00 and 4.00, and 'Expensive' if greater than 4.00.

**Advanced Subquery/Self-Join**: Find all pairs of customers who share the same last name. Display both customers' full names and their last name. (Hint: Ensure you don't list the same person paired with themselves).
